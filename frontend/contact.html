Common Questions

Before you reach out

What size companies do you work with?

We work with funded startups, scale-ups, and mid-market enterprises across healthcare, fintech, logistics, e-commerce, and education. We don't have a headcount minimum — we care about the complexity of the problem, not the size of the org chart.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes — the majority of our clients are in North America, Europe, and the Gulf region. We're remote-first and have operated across time zones since day one.

What happens after the project launches?

Every project includes 6 months of post-launch support — monitoring, debugging, and incremental improvements. This isn't an optional add-on; it's built into every engagement.

How long does a typical engagement take?

Most production agent systems take 6–12 weeks from scoping to go-live. Automation pipelines can be faster (3–6 weeks). Complex multi-agent systems with custom RAG can take 12–16 weeks. We'll give you an honest timeline upfront.

Can you work with our existing infrastructure?

Yes. We build on top of what you have — your databases, APIs, cloud environment, and existing tools. We don't require a greenfield project or a platform migration.
