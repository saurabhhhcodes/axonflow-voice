Our Process

No puzzles. No tricks.
Just real work.

Our hiring process is designed to surface genuine ability quickly. You'll talk to the people you'd actually work with — and the assessment will reflect the work itself.

01

Send your application

Email with your GitHub, past work, or anything that shows how you build. No cover letter required — show us the work.

02

30-minute intro call

A quick conversation about your background, our current work, and whether it's a mutual fit. No gotcha questions.

03

Paid technical assessment

A small real-world problem — the kind we actually solve. Paid for your time, designed to take under 3 hours.

04

Offer or honest feedback

We'll move fast. Either an offer within 48 hours, or specific, useful feedback on why it wasn't a fit — no ghosting.
