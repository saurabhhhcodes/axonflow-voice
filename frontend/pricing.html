Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our pricing

What is included in the Starter plan?↓
The Starter plan includes a basic AI agent with 1 integration, 30 days of post-deployment support, and 1 revision cycle. Ideal for small-scale automation needs.
How does the Growth plan differ from Starter?↓
The Growth plan delivers a multi-agent system with RAG capabilities, 3 integrations, 90 days support, and 3 revisions. It is our most popular choice for mid-market businesses.
Do you offer custom enterprise pricing?↓
Yes. The Enterprise plan is fully custom — you get a full AI ecosystem, unlimited integrations, dedicated team, 6 months support, and unlimited revisions. Contact us for a quote.
How quickly can we get started?↓
After our initial consultation call and requirements sign-off, we typically kick off development within 24–48 hours for Starter and Growth projects.
Can I switch plans later?↓
Absolutely. You can upgrade from Starter to Growth or Enterprise at any time. We will prorate the remaining value and transition your systems seamlessly.
